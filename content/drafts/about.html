Who we are

Laptops HUB is a laptop retailer based in Bahawalpur, Pakistan. This website lists the laptops and accessories we sell, with the recorded configuration of each unit and its price in PKR.

What you can do here

  • Browse laptops by brand, processor, memory, storage, screen size and budget.
  • Read the recorded specifications of each listing and compare up to four laptops side by side.
  • Ask us about a specific model or configuration before you decide.
  • Place a Cash on Delivery order where delivery is available, and follow its status with your order number.

How ordering works

Online orders are paid by Cash on Delivery only. Because stock is not counted automatically, our team confirms that your laptop is available before an order is dispatched, and contacts you if anything needs checking.
