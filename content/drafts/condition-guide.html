How condition is described

Every laptop listing shows the condition we have recorded for that unit, such as new or used. There is no letter grading: read the listing for the specific laptop you are interested in.

What to look at

  • Appearance: marks or wear on the lid, base and palm rest.
  • Display: brightness, marks or pixels, and the recorded resolution and size.
  • Keyboard and trackpad: layout and how keys and the trackpad respond.
  • Ports: the ports the model has, as listed in its specifications.
  • Battery: battery condition varies on used laptops; ask us about the unit you want.
  • Storage and memory: the recorded capacity and type.
  • In the box: whether a charger or accessories are included.

Ask before you order

If a detail matters to you and it is not in the listing, contact us with the product name. Our team can tell you more about the specific unit before you place an order.
