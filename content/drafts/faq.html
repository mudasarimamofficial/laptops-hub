Ordering and payment

How do I order?

Choose a laptop and add it to your cart. At checkout enter your name, mobile number and delivery address. The delivery fee and any COD fee for your area are shown before you submit. After you place the order, our team confirms availability with you before dispatch.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Online orders are paid by Cash on Delivery only. There is no card or online payment on this website.

When do I pay?

You pay the total shown at checkout when your order is delivered. Placing an order does not charge you anything.

What happens after I order?

You get an order number and a private confirmation page. Our team checks that the laptop is available and contacts you before dispatch. You can follow the status on Track order.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us with your order number as soon as possible. Changes and cancellations are only possible before the order is dispatched.

Delivery

Do you deliver to my city?

Enter your city and province at checkout. If we deliver there, the delivery options and fees are shown; if not, checkout tells you so and no order is created. You can still contact us to ask.

How much is delivery?

The exact delivery fee and any Cash on Delivery fee for your area are calculated at checkout and shown before you place the order.

How can I track my order?

Open Track order and enter your order number with the phone number you used at checkout. Courier and tracking details appear once your parcel has been dispatched.

Laptops

Is the laptop in stock?

Availability is confirmed by our team before dispatch. We contact you if a laptop you ordered is no longer available.

Are the laptops new or used?

Each listing states its condition where it is recorded. Many laptops in our catalog are used. Check the listing, or ask us about a specific unit.

How do I know the specifications?

Each listing shows the specifications recorded for that laptop. Anything not recorded is left out rather than guessed. Ask us if you need a detail that is not listed.

Can I compare laptops?

Yes. Add up to four laptops to compare and see their processor, memory, storage, screen and price side by side.
