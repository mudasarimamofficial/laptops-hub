Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Laptops HUB (“we”) handles personal information when you use www.laptopshub.pk to browse, enquire, or place a cash-on-delivery order.

What we collect

When you place an order we collect: your name, mobile number, optional email address, delivery address (lines, city, province, optional postcode and landmark), optional order note, the items and amounts in the order, and technical signals used to prevent abuse (a one-way hash of your IP address and your browser user-agent). Contact and enquiry forms collect the details you enter.

Why we use it

  • To confirm availability, deliver your order, and contact you about it.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse of the website.
  • To keep records required for business and legal duties.

Who processes it

We use website hosting (Vercel), a database service (Supabase), email delivery for order notices when configured (Resend), and the courier that delivers your parcel. We do not sell your personal information for advertising. This website does not use advertising trackers today.

Payment

Cash-on-delivery orders are paid to the courier. We do not collect card numbers on this website for COD.

Retention and your requests

Order records are kept as long as needed for fulfilment, accounting and dispute handling. To ask for a copy or correction of your personal data held for an order, contact us through the Contact page and include your order number and mobile number used at checkout.

Updates

We may update this policy when our systems or legal duties change. The published version on this page is the current one.
