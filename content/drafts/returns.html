Returns and refunds

Laptops HUB sells used and refurbished computers and accessories as described on each product page. We want you to receive what you ordered.

Before you open a return

Contact us with your order number as soon as you notice a problem. Describe the issue and, where useful, send clear photos or a short video. Many issues can be resolved without a return (wrong listing details, missing accessory, transit damage).

When a return may be accepted

  • The item is not the model or configuration shown on the product page you ordered.
  • The item arrives with transit damage that was not disclosed.
  • A fault that makes the product unusable appears promptly after delivery and was not described in the listing.

Change-of-mind returns are not automatically available on used or refurbished laptops. Ask us before returning anything; we will tell you clearly whether a return is possible in your case.

Condition and process

If we approve a return, keep all original accessories and packaging where possible, and do not install irreversible changes. Return shipping responsibility (who pays) will be confirmed in writing for your case before you send the parcel. Refunds, when agreed, are made after we receive and check the item. Because payment was cash on delivery, refund method and timing will be arranged with you directly (for example bank transfer).

Exchanges

Exchanges depend on stock. If we offer an exchange, the price difference (if any) and any new delivery fee will be confirmed before we proceed.
