Where we deliver

Laptops HUB accepts cash-on-delivery (COD) orders for destinations covered by our active shipping zones in Pakistan. At launch, the active zone is All Over PK (nationwide provinces as configured in checkout). If your city cannot be served, checkout will refuse the order before you place it.

Delivery and COD fees

Before you confirm an order, checkout shows:

  • the price of each item,
  • the delivery fee for your selected province/city, and
  • any cash-on-delivery fee.

Current standard charges for the All Over PK zone are Rs 360 delivery and Rs 50 COD fee, unless a future free-delivery threshold or updated zone fee is shown at checkout. The total you see before submitting is the total you pay on delivery. Amounts are calculated on our servers; do not rely on figures typed elsewhere.

How delivery works

An online order is a request. Our team contacts you to confirm that the laptop (or accessory) is still available and that the delivery details are correct before we dispatch. Courier partner and usual transit time can vary by city; we share tracking details on your order page once a parcel is handed to a courier.

If something goes wrong in transit

If a parcel arrives damaged or incomplete, refuse the damaged package where possible and contact us with your order number and photos. We will help arrange a replacement or another fair remedy after we check what happened. Failed delivery attempts or a refused parcel may require a re-attempt; extra courier charges, if any, will be explained before we resend.

Cash on delivery

You pay the full checkout total in cash to the courier when the parcel is delivered. We do not collect card details on this website for COD orders.
