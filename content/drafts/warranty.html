Product condition

Every laptop listing on Laptops HUB shows the configuration we record for that unit (for example processor, memory and storage) and its listed condition. Used and refurbished machines may show normal cosmetic wear. We do not invent stock counts on the website; availability is confirmed with you after you place an order.

What this page covers

This page explains how we handle post-sale support. It is not a manufacturer warranty certificate. Manufacturer coverage, if any still applies to a specific unit, depends on that brand’s own terms and the age of the machine.

After-sale support

If a genuine fault appears that is inconsistent with the listing, contact us with your order number. We will review the case and offer a fair remedy such as guidance, repair arrangement, replacement with a similar unit when available, or another agreed solution. Remedies are decided case by case after we understand the fault.

What is not covered

  • Damage from drops, liquid, misuse, or unauthorized repair.
  • Software issues caused by user-installed programs, account lockouts, or operating-system activation problems after delivery.
  • Normal wear on batteries, which lose capacity over time.
  • Issues not reported promptly after you notice them.

How to request help

Use the contact options on our Contact page, or reply to your order conversation, and include the order number. Do not return a product until we agree the next step.
