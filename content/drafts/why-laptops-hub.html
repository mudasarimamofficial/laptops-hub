What you can rely on

  • Recorded specifications. Each listing shows the processor, memory, storage, display and other details recorded for that laptop. Details that are not recorded are not guessed.
  • Side-by-side comparison. Add up to four laptops and see processor, memory, storage, screen and price in one table, with differences highlighted.
  • Prices in PKR, calculated by the store. At checkout the item price, delivery fee and any COD fee for your area are calculated on our server and shown before you place the order.
  • Cash on Delivery. You pay when your order is delivered, in areas where we offer delivery. There is no online card payment.
  • A person checks your order. Our team confirms availability with you before dispatch, so you are not charged for a laptop we cannot send.
  • Order status you can see. Track your order with its number and the phone number you used at checkout. Courier details appear once your parcel is dispatched.
  • Staff you can reach. Call us or send a message about a model, a configuration or an existing order.
