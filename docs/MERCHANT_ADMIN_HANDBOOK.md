## Customer reviews on the homepage (Catalog → Reviews → Store testimonials)

The homepage section "What our customers say" shows **only reviews you publish here**. With none published, the
section is hidden.

1. **Add testimonial.** Copy the customer's words exactly. Never write, improve or combine reviews.
2. **Where did it come from?**
   - *Posted on Google* — only for a real review on your Google listing. Paste the link to that review (open it on
     Google Maps → Share → Copy link). Only these show the Google "G".
   - *Sent to Laptops HUB* — e.g. a WhatsApp message or email from the customer.
   - *Customer testimonial* — given to you in person or by phone.
3. **Star rating** only if the customer gave one. **Review date** only if you know it.
4. **Customer photo** only with their permission (tick the box).
5. **Consent** — tick only when the customer agreed to appear on the website. Publishing needs it.
6. **Internal note** (never shown publicly): how you checked it, e.g. "Order LH-1042, agreed on WhatsApp 12 Nov".
7. Save as draft → **Submit for approval** → an owner, admin or manager presses **Approve and publish**.
   Editors can prepare and submit but cannot publish.
8. **Hide (unpublish)** removes it from the homepage at once and can be undone. **Delete** is permanent (a copy stays
   in the history); prefer Hide.
9. **History** on each entry shows who added, edited, submitted, published or hid it, and when.

The homepage never shows an overall star average or review count, and does not copy reviews from Google.

## Homepage sections (Online store → Customize)

- **Customer reviews**: on/off, heading, short intro, **Position** (before the enquiry form, after featured laptops,
  or after the comparison section), and the "Our profile on Google" link (leave empty to hide the button).
- **Store map**: on/off, heading, intro, the Google Maps embed link and "Open in Google Maps" link (only Google Maps
  links are accepted), the reveal effect, and "click to load" (the map loads only when a visitor asks).
- Other homepage sections keep the fixed V5 order by design.
- Changes are a draft until you **Publish**.

## Choosing many products at once (Catalog → Products)

Tick the boxes beside products (or the box in the header for the whole page). A bar appears:
**Set active · Set draft · Archive · Feature · Unfeature · Edit prices in bulk editor · Clear selection.**
Draft and Archive ask first; they hide products from the store (orders already placed never change). "Edit prices in
bulk editor" opens just the ticked products for price/status edits.

## Admin speed

Pages now load in well under a second in the same region (see `ADMIN_PERFORMANCE_REPORT.md`). If a page ever feels
slow again, note the page and time and tell engineering.
