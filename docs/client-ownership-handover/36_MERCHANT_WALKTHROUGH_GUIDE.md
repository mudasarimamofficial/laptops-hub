# 36 — Your 14-step store walkthrough (owner guide)

This guide is for the store owner. It takes about 60–90 minutes. You do it yourself, signed in with your own owner
account at `https://www.laptopshub.pk/admin`. Nobody else needs your password, and nobody from engineering will ask
for it.

**Why:** these are the 14 jobs you will do most often. Doing each one once proves the store is yours to run. Your
notes ("was it clear?") tell engineering what to make simpler.

## Before you start (5 minutes)

1. Sign in at `/admin/login`.
2. Go to **Settings → Backups** and click **Create backup**. Name it `Before walkthrough`.
3. Keep a notes file open. For each step, write:
   - **Clear?** yes or no;
   - what confused you, if anything.

**Safety rules:**
- Use **one new draft product** called `Walkthrough test` for steps 1–3. Delete it at the end.
- Use **one draft blog guide** for step 7.
- Never type real customer details into a test.
- Step 9 (recording cash) is done **only** for a real order whose cash you really received. If there is none yet,
  skip step 9 and come back to it.
- If anything looks wrong, stop and write down what you saw. Nothing in this list is hard to undo.

## The 14 steps

### 1. Add a laptop with variants and specifications — *Products → Add product*
1. Name it `Walkthrough test`. Leave status as **Draft**.
2. Add two variants (for example two RAM sizes), each with its own price and stock.
3. Fill in a few specifications. Save.
4. **Check:** open the storefront address of the product in a private window. It must **not** be visible (it is a
   draft).

### 2. Upload and reorder its photos — *the product → Media*
1. Upload two photos you own. Give each one alt text (what the photo shows).
2. Drag the second photo to first place. Save.
3. **Check:** the first photo is marked as the main photo.

### 3. Edit its search appearance — *the product → Search appearance*
1. Type a search title and description.
2. **Check:** the Google preview on the page updates as you type. Save.

### 4. Add and manage a collection — *Collections*
1. Create a collection. Pick products by hand **or** with a rule.
2. Change the order of two products. Save.
3. **Check:** open the collection on the storefront. The products appear in your order.
4. Delete the collection afterwards, unless you want to keep it.

### 5. Update homepage content — *Online Store (Customize)*
1. Change one homepage text. Click **Save draft**.
2. Click **Preview**. Your change shows only in the preview, not on the live site.
3. Either **Publish**, or **Discard** the draft.
4. If you published: open **Versions** and **Restore** the previous one. The homepage goes back.

### 6. Change navigation — *Navigation*
1. Rename one menu link. Save. Saved menu changes go live straight away.
2. **Check:** the desktop header, the phone menu and the footer.
3. Open the version history and **restore** the previous menu.

### 7. Create a blog guide — *Content → Blog*
1. Write a short draft guide. Save it as a draft.
2. **Check:** it does not appear under **Guides** on the storefront.
3. Delete the draft, or publish it only if it is real content you want customers to read.

### 8. Review an order — *Orders → open an order*
1. Open the most recent real order.
2. **Check:** items, variant, delivery fee and total match what the customer was shown.
3. Do not change its status unless you are really processing it.

### 9. Record a COD collection — *the order → Record collection*
**Only** for a delivered order whose cash you have in hand.
1. Enter the amount actually received.
2. **Check:** the order shows the collection.
3. If you made a mistake, record a **correction**. A correction is a new entry; the original stays in the history.

### 10. Handle an inquiry — *Inquiries*
1. Open an inquiry. Change its status and add a note.
2. **Check:** the note is saved.

### 11. Change contact information — *Settings → Contact & social*
1. Look at the phone, WhatsApp and address.
2. Change something only if it is genuinely wrong. Save.
3. **Check:** the footer and the WhatsApp button on the storefront.
4. See decision O3 in doc 23: should the address read "Shop no 3"?

### 12. Manage notifications — *Settings → Notifications*
1. Check the staff alert address is a mailbox you read.
2. Click **Send test**. **Check:** the email arrives (look in spam too).
3. Leave customer email as it is, unless you decide to change it.

### 13. Create and check a backup — *Settings → Backups*
1. Create a backup named `After walkthrough`.
2. Download it and read its summary.
3. **Do not restore** on the live store unless you really need to undo something.

### 14. Edit and restore a theme draft — *Online Store (Customize) → Site-wide text*
1. Change one site-wide text. Save draft and preview it.
2. Discard the draft, or publish and then restore the earlier version.

## When you finish (5 minutes)

1. Delete the `Walkthrough test` product, and any test collection or draft guide.
2. Go to **Activity** and search `walkthrough` or your email. Every step you took should be listed.
3. Send your notes to engineering. "Clear: yes/no" for each step is enough.

**What this proves:** you can run the everyday store without help. It does **not** cover the first real COD order,
which has its own checklist (`37`).
