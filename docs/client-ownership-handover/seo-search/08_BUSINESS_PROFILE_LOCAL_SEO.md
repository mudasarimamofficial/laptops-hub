**Owner checklist**
1. business.google.com → confirm you own the profile. Do **not** create a second one.
2. Website field = `https://www.laptopshub.pk`.
3. The name, phone and address match Admin → Settings → Contact & Social exactly.
4. Add opening hours there (and tell us if they should be shown on the site too).
5. Real photos; reply to reviews; never solicit fake reviews.

The site markup intentionally has no hours, map coordinates or ratings until the owner supplies real values (ratings never belong in self-markup).
