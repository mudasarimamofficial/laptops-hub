# 09 — Answer-engine (AEO) / generative-engine (GEO) strategy

Honest scope: there is no tag or file (including `llms.txt`) that guarantees AI assistants will cite a site. They draw on the same signals as search: crawlable pages, clear entity facts, and useful, original content.

**Done**
- A single consistent entity: the same name, address, phones and socials in visible text and in JSON-LD, with `sameAs` links to the official social profiles.
- Readable H1 and an explicit brand + city homepage title and description.
- Guides marked up as Articles with real dates and the publisher.

**Recommended (owner content decisions, not invented by engineering)**
1. Guides that answer real customer questions (for example, choosing a used business laptop in Pakistan, or what i5 8th vs 11th gen means), written from the shop's own experience.
2. A factual "About Laptops HUB" page: when it opened, where it is, and what it sells. Only owner-supplied facts.
3. Policy pages (returns, warranty, delivery). These must be written by the owner. They are also needed for Merchant Center.
4. Keep the Business Profile active: photos, posts and replies to reviews.

**Not done, deliberately**: mass AI landing pages, fake FAQs, invented expert quotes, keyword-stuffed city pages.
