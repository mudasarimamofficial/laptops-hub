# 15 — Client SEO operations guide (plain language)

## What Google can and cannot do for you
- Google decides **when** it visits the site, **what** it shows and **where** it ranks. Nobody can guarantee a position, including us, an agency or Google Ads.
- What you control: true, consistent business details; clear product pages; no duplicate listings; and a verified Search Console.

## Weekly (10 minutes)
1. Open **Search Console** (search.google.com/search-console) with the business Google account. Look at *Performance → Queries* for "laptops hub" searches.
2. Open **Admin → SEO**. Fix anything in *Products sharing one search title*.
3. When you add a product, check that the title has brand, model, CPU, RAM and storage. The search title is built from these automatically.

## When you add or change a product
- Nothing to submit: the sitemap updates by itself, and Google finds new products on its next visit.
- For an important new product, open Search Console → *URL Inspection* → paste the product URL → *Request indexing* (a daily limit applies).

## When you archive a product
- Its page stops appearing in the sitemap, and Google drops it over time. No action is needed.

## When the website is not appearing on Google
1. Search Console → *URL Inspection* for the homepage. Is it "URL is on Google"?
2. If it says "not on Google", click *Request indexing*.
3. Search Console → *Pages* report: look for errors (server error, blocked, noindex). Send any errors to your developer.
4. Check that https://www.laptopshub.pk opens normally.

## Business Profile (Google Maps)
- Keep the name, address, phone and website **exactly the same** as Admin → Settings → Contact & Social.
- Add real photos and reply to reviews. Never buy or write fake reviews.

## Changing the homepage search text
Admin → SEO → *Homepage search appearance*. Use only true facts. Google may still choose its own wording.
